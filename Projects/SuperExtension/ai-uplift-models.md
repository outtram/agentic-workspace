# AI Uplift Models

**Baseline:** 10-person squad · 2,000 story points / year velocity

---

## 1. How Extreme Do We Go With AI?

A spectrum of three positions:

1. **Minimal — Nithin Embedded in Squad 2**
   - Nithin replaces an offshore API dev (likely senior consultant)
   - He basically *builds the rocket ship* on the job
   - Lowest disruption, lowest risk

2. **Middle Ground**
   - Something in between minimal and all-in
   - *(to be defined)*

3. **ALL IN — 5-Person AI-First Squad**
   - Fully AI-native squad of 5, targeted at Squad 2 scope
   - Builds the rocket ship from scratch
   - **Note:** Work done in Squad 2 over time could compound to benefit Squad 1 and Squad 3 — bigger upside potential

---

## 2. Finance Models

Three ways to structure the commercial arrangement:

1. **Fixed Cost (same as Squad 2)**
   - We do all the work, we take all the risk — and all the rewards if any

2. **50/50 Investment Model**
   - We invest 50/50 in the cost of embedding AI builders (e.g. Nithin) or Agentic Engineering Enabled Testers
   - Example: Replace 4 testers with 2 AI-enabled testers → split the savings 50/50
   - Charge a premium on AI testers (they are ~2× as efficient as before)

3. **Aus Super Pays for Uplift**
   - Aus Super funds the cost of building the Agents/Skills
   - They receive the benefit as increased output
   - Estimated output uplift: **5–10%**
